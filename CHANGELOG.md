# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-12)

The first stable version. Later 1.x versions may add features, but they won't break programs
written for this one.

### The language

- Variables named by their last word, whole-number arithmetic, conditions including `we're at`,
  `If` / `That said`, `Per my last email` loops and `Bumping this.`
- People in the quoted thread as functions, with `Net-net`, takes, `the ask` and offline scopes
- Out-of-office error handling, attachments and reply-all
- Jargon, mail merge, hiring freezes, the backlog, pronouns, timeboxes and random estimates
- `FYI` and `P.S.` comments
- Politeness checks when HR is cc'd
- Character input and output with `Let me spell it out.` and `Reading between the lines.`
- Autocorrect with `Sent from my iPhone`

### The interpreter

- Runs saved `.eml` files, including HTML-only emails and attachments inside the email
- Understands Outlook-style and forwarded threads as well as `>` quoting
- `--version`
- 17 example programs and a saved email, including a Brainfuck interpreter
